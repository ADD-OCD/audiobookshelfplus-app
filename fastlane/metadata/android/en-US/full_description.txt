<p>Audiobookshelf+ is an unofficial Android app for your self-hosted Audiobookshelf server. It is an independent fork of the official Audiobookshelf mobile app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Audiobookshelf project. Audiobookshelf is a free and open source audiobook and podcast server.</p><p>Connect to your server and stream or download your audiobooks and podcasts, with continuous series and collection playback, an Up Next queue, playback restoration and a home screen widget.</p>
